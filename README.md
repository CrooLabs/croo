# Croo

**Your persistent AI agent crew.** Croo runs a team of named AI agents
on your own machine — each with its own name, job and memory of
yesterday — and gives you one workspace to brief them, watch them work,
and decide what goes ahead.

[screenshot: the workspace in full — fleet grid on the left, two agent
panes side by side, one agent working and one waiting, usage rings
visible]

Agents run on the engines you already have — Claude, Codex, AGY and
Grok, side by side — and their replies all read the same way, in one
clean chat. They discuss plans with each other and land on a written
consensus you can read. They hand work to each other through mailboxes
you can open. And one of them, **Nova**, is your custodian: she sets
Croo up, keeps it healthy, and answers questions about everything in
these pages.

That's the standing offer of this whole repository:
**inside Croo? Just ask Nova.** The docs are here for before you
install, for reading at your own pace, and for the rare day something
won't load.
