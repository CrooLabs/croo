# Support  (DRAFT 1, 2026-10-12)

*Croo Docs · 2026-10-12*

This page is for two situations: something is stuck and you've already
tried Nova and [the emergency page](cli.md) — or Croo is running fine
and you've simply found something worth telling us about. Both are
welcome. Croo is in beta, and reports like yours are how it gets good.

## The easy way: let Nova write the report

If you can reach Nova at all, don't write the report yourself. One
sentence does it:

> "Nova, write up a report of what just happened, for Croo support."

She'll assemble what happened, when, and the details that matter, far
more reliably than memory does. Read it, take out anything you'd
rather keep private, and send it (below).

**The workspace being down doesn't take Nova with it.** If the chat
won't load, open a terminal and type `croo attach nova` — same Nova,
same memory — and ask her for the report there. Copy it out of the
terminal, and leave with Ctrl-b, then d.

## The by-hand version, when Nova herself is down

Collecting takes minutes. In your own words:

- **What happened** — what you expected, what you got instead.
- **What you tried** — which story from [the emergency page](cli.md),
  and where it stopped helping.
- **The exact wording** of any error message, word for word.
- **Your setup** — Croo version, and Mac or Ubuntu.
- **Roughly when** it happened, so we can find it from our end.

## What a report should never contain

- **No passwords, tokens or key files** — we will never ask for them,
  and a report containing one will be deleted on sight.
- **Your conversations are yours.** If an excerpt shows the bug,
  share the excerpt — not the whole transcript. Read anything Nova
  drafts before sending; she writes for usefulness, you decide
  what leaves your machine.

## Where to send it

- The **feedback form** at [croolabs.com](https://croolabs.com) — the
  same one the site's Feedback page describes.
- Or email **support@croolabs.com**.

## What to expect back

We read everything. You'll hear back from a person, and if your report
leads to a fix, we'll tell you when it ships. Croo is in beta — we
won't promise response times we haven't earned yet, but reports never
disappear into a void.

Inside Croo? Just ask Nova.
