# 9 · Croo away from the desk

*Croo Operator's Manual · v1.0.1 · 2026-10-13*

**The one thing to know: the crew keeps working when you leave — and
your word can still reach them.** Croo follows you two independent
ways, and they're worth telling apart from the start.

## Your phone, two ways

- **The Croo web client** — the workspace itself, in a layout built
  for phones. The full check-in: see your agents, read replies, speak,
  listen, answer. Because your phone is talking to your machine, this
  branch needs your machine reachable first — that's its own short
  doc, **Reach Croo from anywhere**, and it's worth doing early.
- **Telegram** — a lightweight line to your crew through the Telegram
  app you already have. No network setup at all: the connection goes
  outward from your machine to Telegram, so it works from anywhere
  the moment it's set up.

Use either, or both: the web client when you want the workspace in
your pocket, Telegram when you want your crew in your messages.

## The web client

Croo's phone layout: an agent picker, the chat, quick keys and
one-tap answers. Open it in the phone's browser and add it to your
home screen — it behaves like an app from then on. It's deliberately
simpler than the desktop: on the phone you're checking in and giving
your word, not conducting the whole operation.

Three things the web client does on the phone:

- **Talk instead of type.** Tap the microphone and speak; your words
  become text on your own machine — speech never leaves your desk to
  be transcribed. English only for now.
- **Hear the reply.** A button reads out a short spoken summary of an
  agent's latest reply, in a natural Microsoft voice. The glance-free
  check-in.
- **Driving mode.** Hands free: speaking sends your message, and
  replies are read out. Driving, cycling, cooking — your pick. A
  commute becomes a check-in, not a blackout.

## Telegram

Ask Nova to set it up — she walks you through it step by step, until
the first message lands on your phone.

From then on, your Telegram chat is a line to the crew:

- **`/bind jack`** — talk to Jack directly: ask for status, send the
  next instruction, answer his questions. **`/unbind`** and the chat
  is a private scratchpad again — notes to yourself that go nowhere.
- **A question from an agent arrives with its choices** — tap one, or
  reply with its number. The same one-click answers as the workspace,
  in your messages.
- **`/status`** — the agent list at a glance. **`/voice on`** — spoken
  replies, and you can speak too: voice goes both ways.
  **`/powerthrough`** — routine approvals handled for five minutes,
  from wherever you are. **`/help`** — the full list.

And the part that changes your day: when you're away, your agents and
your cues reach you here. The question that would have sat in
**Waiting for you** until evening gets answered from the lunch queue,
and the crew keeps moving.

## What reaches you, and how to quiet it

Away from the desk, the traffic is yours to shape: which alerts follow
you, what Telegram carries, and when everything stays quiet. The mute
button silences the desk; put your phone on do-not-disturb and Croo
doesn't fight it. The crew works either way — what you're choosing is
when to be told, not whether they continue.
