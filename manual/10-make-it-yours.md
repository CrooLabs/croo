# 10 · Make it yours

*Croo Operator's Manual · v1.0.1 · 2026-10-13*

**The one thing to know: the workspace adapts to you, not the other
way round.** None of this chapter is required — it's the set of dials
that make a long day in Croo feel like your own desk.

## Looks

Three themes — Dark, Matrix and Ubuntu — plus your choice of font,
zoom level, and what each pane header shows. Got a theme in mind?
Suggest it, and we may add it.

## Keys

Keyboard shortcuts cover the moves you make constantly: switch agents,
send to a pane, flip layouts, open the work map. Behind them sits a
full key library in the menu — browse it, and pin up to 14 keystrokes
or short texts to the bar at the bottom of the screen. The bar is
yours: the keys you actually use, one row, always there.

## Cues

Croo calls its reminders cues. The built-in ones look after you — eye
breaks, water, stretching — if you listen. Better yet, make your own:
a cue can say anything, on your schedule, and reach you like any other
alert (chapter 7) — or on your phone when you're away (chapter 9).

## Notes

Your own notepad inside the workspace: pin notes, reorder them, search
them, or pop one out as a sticky. It's deliberately yours alone —
agents never write in it. Thoughts land here while agents work;
briefs get built from thoughts.

## The Playground

For the kids ;) — a few small games shipped with Croo, for while an
agent works or when you need to switch off. A guide shows you how to
build your own, local AI included, and any single-file page you add
appears in the menu: games, status boards, whatever you like.

## The local AI

Through Ollama, an assistant runs on your own machine — and once it's
running, Croo quietly hands it the small jobs: shortcuts that turn
long text into commands, quick math, Tidy my draft, Read the room's
one-liners, the live discussion commentary, parts of the Playground.
Nothing it touches leaves your desk. One-time setup; ask Nova.

Inside Croo? Just ask Nova.
